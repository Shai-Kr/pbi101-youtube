# Bean There Coffee Co. - business context

- **Who uses this report:** Maya (COO) and our 8 store managers, every Monday at 9am. They decide staffing, promos and stock for the week.
- **Fiscal year starts July 1.** FY27 = Jul 1, 2026 - Jun 30, 2027. "YTD" always means fiscal YTD.
- **Net Margin** = Net Sales - COGS - delivery commission. The delivery apps keep **20%** of Delivery-channel net sales. Managers never see gross margin.
- **Same-store sales:** only stores open at least 13 full months before the period starts. Beltline (Sep 2025) and Union Station (Mar 2026) don't count yet.
- **Avg Ticket** = Net Sales / Tickets. Net Sales already excludes tax and the 5% mobile-app discount.
- **Targets:** Net Margin >= 68%. Same-store growth >= 5% vs last year.
- **Naming:** measures in Title Case, no abbreviations (no "GM"). Money in CAD, no decimals except per-ticket values.
- **Brand colors:** espresso #3B2A20, crema #F3E9DC, copper #C8743A; good = mint #2BB673, bad = berry #C2185B.
